 # Repository: AddonBHVinebound

This repository houses the addon “Vinebound” for the "Blue Hearts" Minecraft mod.

## Minecraft versions supported
* 26.1.2.

## Additions:
* Poisonous Vital Fruit: Rarely obtained by harvesting Vital Bushes. After eating it, vines spread across your body, unleashing Goodfellow's Vine Armor;
* Goodfellow’s Vine Armor: Grants you two disadvantages and two advantages:
    * When it catches fire, it burns indefinitely;
    * Reduces damage taken by 25%;
    * When entering water, removes all harmful effects;
    * Unlocks the Vine Armor skill tree.
* Vine Armor skill tree:
    * Earn EXP and Points by: eating Vital Fruit, harvesting crops, and earning EXP in Minecraft;
    * Spend Points to gain skills such as: looting, block range, fortune, water breathing, damage reflection, and more.
